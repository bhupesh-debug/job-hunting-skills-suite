# Red Flags & STEM OPT Compliance Checklist

---

## 🚩 Red Flag #1: Third-Party Consulting / Bench Placement Model

**What it looks like:**
- A consulting firm (often IT staffing) hires you as their "employee" but places you at a client site
- The E-Verify employer is the staffing firm, but your actual supervisor is at the client
- Common in IT staffing: Infosys BPM, Wipro, Cognizant BPS, smaller desi staffing shops

**Why it's risky:**
- USCIS requires that the E-Verify employer and the "employer of record" be the same entity providing supervision and training
- If you're placed at a third-party client site, and that client is not the E-Verify employer, you may be out of status
- USCIS has audited and revoked OPT for workers in these arrangements

**How to detect it:**
- Ask: "Will I be working at [Company]'s own office, or will I be placed at a client site?"
- Ask: "Who will sign my Form I-983 Training Plan — someone at your company or the end client?"
- Ask: "Is [Company] the legal employer of record AND the E-Verify account holder?"

**Rule:** If work is performed at a third-party client site and the E-Verify employer is a staffing intermediary → **Avoid unless legal counsel confirms compliance.**

---

## 🚩 Red Flag #2: Employer Refuses to Share E-Verify Employer ID Number

**What it looks like:**
- HR says "we're E-Verify enrolled, trust us" but won't provide the Employer ID or Company ID from USCIS
- Employer says "we'll deal with that paperwork after you start"

**Why it's risky:**
- Your DSO needs the E-Verify Company ID to report your STEM OPT
- If an employer can't provide it, they may not actually be enrolled
- Starting work before E-Verify is confirmed = potential unlawful employment

**What to do:**
- Ask HR: "Could you share your E-Verify Company ID number so my DSO can verify enrollment?"
- Cross-check at: https://www.e-verify.uscis.gov/env/EmployerSearchForm.aspx
- If they refuse or delay → **Do not accept the offer until resolved.**

## 🚩 Red Flag #3: Unpaid or Below-Market Wage Offers

**What it looks like:**
- "Unpaid internship converting to full-time" — **100% illegal under STEM OPT**
- Suspiciously low salary (e.g., $25K/year for a data science role in NYC)
- Pay in "equity only" or "stipend only" structures

**Why it's risky:**
- STEM OPT requires that you receive compensation that is "commensurate with that of U.S. workers in similar positions"
- Unpaid employment violates both STEM OPT regulations and DOL wage rules
- USCIS can revoke OPT authorization and place you out of status

**Market wage benchmarks for NYC (2024–2025):**
| Role | Acceptable Minimum | Typical Range |
|------|--------------------|---------------|
| Data Analyst | $65,000 | $75K–$95K |
| Data Scientist | $90,000 | $110K–$145K |
| Software Engineer | $95,000 | $120K–$160K |
| Quant Analyst | $100,000 | $120K–$175K |
| Financial Analyst | $70,000 | $80K–$110K |
| Audit/Accounting Analyst | $60,000 | $70K–$90K |

**Rule:** If the offer is below these thresholds, negotiate up or decline.

## 🚩 Red Flag #4: Employer Unwilling to Complete Form I-983

**What it looks like:**
- HR says "we don't do that paperwork"
- Employer says "just tell your school whatever you need"
- Employer wants you to fill in your own supervisor's section

**Why it's risky:**
- Form I-983 must be completed honestly and signed by an authorized employer representative
- You (the student) and your DSO must also sign it
- Falsified I-983 = immigration fraud

**What to say:**
> "The I-983 Training Plan is a 2-page form required by USCIS for my STEM OPT authorization. It simply describes my job duties and learning goals — it takes about 20–30 minutes to complete. Would HR or my future supervisor be able to assist?"

If they refuse → this employer cannot legally employ a STEM OPT worker. Walk away.

---

## 🚩 Red Flag #5: Role Has No Clear Relationship to STEM Degree

**What it looks like:**
- A CS graduate being hired as a "sales associate" or "account manager" with no data/tech component
- An accounting graduate hired as a "customer service rep"
- Vague job titles like "project coordinator" or "operations associate" with unclear duties

**Why it's risky:**
- STEM OPT requires the job to be "directly related" to your degree field
- Your DSO evaluates this; if USCIS audits, you need documentation
- Misaligned roles can get your OPT revoked retroactively

**Rule:** Ensure the job description includes tasks that map clearly to your degree field. Ask for a written job description before signing.

---

## ✅ STEM OPT Compliance Checklist (Before Accepting Any Offer)

- [ ] Employer is enrolled in E-Verify (verify ID at USCIS portal)
- [ ] Employer can provide E-Verify Company ID to your DSO
- [ ] Employer will sign Form I-983 Training Plan
- [ ] Role is degree-related (documented in I-983)
- [ ] Compensation meets or exceeds fair market wage
- [ ] Named supervisor identified with direct oversight responsibility
- [ ] You are employed directly (not placed at a third-party worksite)
- [ ] DSO has been notified and your SEVIS record updated before start date
- [ ] 6-month and 12-month evaluation reports scheduled in advance
