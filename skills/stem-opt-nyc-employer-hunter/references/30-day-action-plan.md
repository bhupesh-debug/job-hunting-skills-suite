# 30-Day STEM OPT Job Conversion Action Plan (NYC)

**Goal:** Land an offer at an E-Verify–enrolled employer within 30 days.
**Daily minimum:** 10 applications + 3 networking touchpoints.

---

## Pre-Launch Checklist (Day 0 — Before Starting)

- [ ] Resume updated with STEM OPT status language ("authorized to work — no sponsorship required for 3 years")
- [ ] LinkedIn profile optimized: headline includes role + "STEM OPT | Open to Work"
- [ ] DSO consulted — confirm OPT authorization start date and any pending deadlines
- [ ] Job tracker spreadsheet set up (columns: Company, Role, Date Applied, Contact, E-Verify Status, Follow-up Date, Status)
- [ ] E-Verify employer lookup bookmarked: https://www.e-verify.uscis.gov/env/EmployerSearchForm.aspx
- [ ] LinkedIn Premium activated (Career plan — free trial available)

---

## Week 1 (Days 1–7): Foundation + Volume

**Theme:** Build pipeline. Get 70+ applications out.

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Apply to 10 roles from the employer list (Big Tech + Big 4 first). Set up job alerts on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor. |
| 2 | Apply 10 more. Identify 5 recruiters at target companies. Send 3 LinkedIn connection requests. |
| 3 | Apply 10. Cold email 2 HR contacts using the outreach template. Verify E-Verify status for all applied-to employers. |
| 4 | Apply 10. Attend 1 virtual networking event or career fair (check Handshake, Eventbrite). |
| 5 | Apply 10. Send 3 LinkedIn messages to alumni at target companies. Ask for informational interviews. |
| 6 | Review application responses. Follow up on any ghosted applications (wait 5 business days). Research 10 new companies. |
| 7 | Rest + review. Update job tracker. Identify Week 1 wins and gaps. |

**Week 1 Targets:**
- 70 applications submitted
- 10 recruiter connections on LinkedIn
- 3 informational interviews scheduled
- 1 networking event attended

---

## Week 2 (Days 8–14): Networking + Interviews

**Theme:** Convert connections to conversations.

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 8 | Apply 10. Conduct informational interview #1. Ask: team structure, OPT history, E-Verify status. |
| 9 | Apply 10. Follow up on Week 1 applications. Send 3 more LinkedIn messages. |
| 10 | Apply 10. Conduct informational interview #2. Take notes; ask for referrals. |
| 11 | Apply 10. If recruiter screen scheduled: prep STAR stories (2 min each) for top 5 behavioral questions. |
| 12 | Apply 10. Recruiter screens / phone interviews if any. Practice data/coding problems (LeetCode Easy-Medium) or finance case problems. |
| 13 | Apply 10. Attend 1 in-person networking event or alumni mixer in NYC (check Columbia, NYU, Baruch alumni networks). |
| 14 | Review. Refine resume based on feedback. Update LinkedIn. Follow up on all pending applications. |

**Week 2 Targets:**
- 70 more applications (total: 140)
- 2–3 informational interviews completed
- 1–2 recruiter screens
- 3+ referrals requested from new contacts

---

## Week 3 (Days 15–21): Interview Prep + Escalation

**Theme:** Push pipeline to offer stage.

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 15 | Apply 10. Full technical mock interview (data case, SQL query, or coding problem depending on role). |
| 16 | Apply 10. Behavioral interview mock (STAR format; record yourself on Loom or Zoom). Review playback. |
| 17 | Apply 10. Company-specific research for any companies with interviews pending. Prep 5 thoughtful questions for each. |
| 18 | Apply 10. Reach out to 3 more alumni/contacts at pending-interview companies. |
| 19 | Apply 10. Phone/video interviews if scheduled. Immediately send thank-you emails within 1 hour of each interview. |
| 20 | Apply 10. Reach back to Week 1 connections who haven't responded — one follow-up is professional and expected. |
| 21 | Review. Identify which companies have gone silent (>10 business days). Decide: follow up or move on. |

**Week 3 Targets:**
- 60+ more applications (rolling total: 200+)
- 3–5 first-round interviews completed
- 1–2 second-round interviews scheduled
- All thank-you emails sent within 24 hours

---

## Week 4 (Days 22–30): Close + Offer Management

**Theme:** Get to offer. Negotiate. Confirm E-Verify. Accept.

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 22 | Final-round interview prep: case study, whiteboard, or presentation as required. |
| 23 | Final-round interviews if scheduled. |
| 24 | Apply 10 more (maintain pipeline even if close to offer). |
| 25 | Follow up with any employers who have given verbal interest but no formal offer. |
| 26 | If offer received: confirm E-Verify enrollment BEFORE accepting. Ask for E-Verify Company ID. |
| 27 | Negotiate salary using market benchmarks (see red-flags-and-compliance.md). |
| 28 | DSO consultation — notify DSO of offer details, confirm I-983 process, set start date. |
| 29 | Accept offer in writing. Get official offer letter with job title, salary, start date, supervisor name. |
| 30 | Submit I-983 to DSO. Confirm SEVIS update. Celebrate. |

| Role | Key Prep Areas |

## Networking Targets (NYC-Specific)

- **Columbia University alumni network** — strong finance + quant pipeline
- **NYU Stern / NYU Tandon alumni** — finance + tech
- **Cornell Tech (NYC)** — tech + startup connections
- **LinkedIn NYC groups** — "NYC Data Science," "NYC Fintech Network," "F-1 OPT Job Seekers"

- **Handshake** — university-verified job postings with OPT filters
- **Wellfound (AngelList)** — startup roles with visa status filters

---

## Daily Cadence Template

```
Morning (1 hour):   Apply to 10 jobs (job boards + company sites)
Midday (30 min):    Send 3 LinkedIn messages / cold emails
Afternoon (1 hour): Interview prep OR informational interview
Evening (30 min):   Update job tracker; research next day's targets
```

**Weekly metrics to track:**
- Applications sent: target 70/week
- Response rate: should reach 5–10% by Week 2
- Interviews: target 2–3/week by Week 3
- Offers: target 1+ by Day 30
