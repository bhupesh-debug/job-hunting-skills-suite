# Targeting Strategy: LinkedIn Search, ATS Keywords, Outreach Scripts

---

## 1. LinkedIn Boolean Search Strings

Use these in LinkedIn Jobs search bar. Combine with location = "New York City Metropolitan Area".

### Finance / Quant
```
("data analyst" OR "quantitative analyst" OR "risk analyst") AND ("New York" OR "NYC") AND ("financial services" OR "fintech" OR "asset management")
```

### Data Science / ML / AI
```
("data scientist" OR "machine learning engineer" OR "ML engineer") AND "New York" AND ("full-time" OR "new grad" OR "entry level")
```

### Software Engineering
```
("software engineer" OR "backend engineer" OR "data engineer") AND "New York City" AND ("Python" OR "SQL" OR "Spark") -"security clearance"
```

### Consulting / Advisory (filter out clearance roles)
```
("business analyst" OR "data analyst" OR "strategy analyst") AND ("consulting" OR "advisory") AND "New York" -"clearance" -"TS/SCI"
```

### Big 4 Accounting / Audit Analytics
```
("audit analytics" OR "data analyst" OR "forensic analyst") AND ("PwC" OR "Deloitte" OR "EY" OR "KPMG") AND "New York"
```

### Startup / Fintech
```
("data scientist" OR "financial analyst" OR "ML engineer") AND ("Series B" OR "Series C" OR "fintech" OR "startup") AND "New York" AND "full-time"
```

## 2. ATS Resume Keywords (by role type)

> Include these naturally throughout your resume — in skills, experience bullets, and summary. ATS parses for exact matches.

### Data Science / Analytics
`Python, SQL, R, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, A/B Testing, Statistical Modeling, Machine Learning, Data Visualization, Tableau, Power BI, Jupyter, Git, ETL, Data Pipeline, Feature Engineering, Regression, Classification, NLP`

### Quant / Finance Analytics
`Python, R, MATLAB, Financial Modeling, Monte Carlo Simulation, Risk Analysis, Portfolio Optimization, Bloomberg Terminal, SQL, VBA, Options Pricing, Derivatives, Quantitative Research, Stochastic Calculus, Backtesting`

### Software Engineering
`Python, Java, C++, JavaScript, TypeScript, REST APIs, Microservices, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, GCP, Azure, CI/CD, Agile, Scrum, React, Node.js, System Design, Data Structures, Algorithms`

### Accounting / Audit Analytics
`Excel, SQL, ACL Analytics, Tableau, Power BI, SAP, Oracle, GAAP, IFRS, Internal Controls, Risk Assessment, SOX Compliance, Data Analytics, Python, CPA (if applicable)`

### Pro-tip: Add these phrases to your resume summary/objective
- "Authorized to work in the U.S. (F-1 STEM OPT, no sponsorship required for 3 years)"
- "Eligible for STEM OPT extension — E-Verify–enrolled employer required"

## 3. HR Outreach Scripts

### LinkedIn Connection Request (short — 300 chars max)
```
Hi [Name], I'm a [degree] grad from [University] currently on STEM OPT. I noticed [Company] is a strong fit for my background in [skill area]. I'd love to connect and learn more about opportunities on your team. Thank you!
```

### LinkedIn InMail / Cold Message (after connecting)
```
Hi [Name],

I'm a [MS/BS] in [Field] from [University], currently on F-1 STEM OPT authorization (eligible to work for 3 years with no employer sponsorship required for the first 3 years).

I'm very interested in [Company]'s work in [specific area]. My background includes [2–3 specific skills or projects], and I believe I could contribute meaningfully to your [team/project].

I wanted to ask: Is [Company] currently enrolled in E-Verify? This is a requirement on my end for STEM OPT. If so, I'd love to discuss open roles or upcoming opportunities.

Happy to share my resume or set up a quick call at your convenience.

Best,
[Your Name]
[LinkedIn URL] | [Email]
```

### Cold Email to Recruiter (subject line + body)
**Subject:** STEM OPT Candidate — [Role] — [University] [Degree]

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I'm a recent [MS in Data Science] graduate from [University]. I'm currently on F-1 STEM OPT status, which allows me to work full-time for up to 3 years without requiring employer visa sponsorship.

I'm reaching out because [Company] appeared frequently in my research as an E-Verify–enrolled employer with strong [data/finance/tech] hiring. I would love to be considered for any open [Data Scientist / Analyst / Engineer] roles.

A quick note on process: As part of STEM OPT compliance, I would need my employer to be enrolled in E-Verify and willing to complete a brief Form I-983 Training Plan — both are standard and require minimal time on the employer's part.

I've attached my resume for your review. Would you be open to a 15-minute conversation?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email] | [LinkedIn]
```

## 4. Recruiter Identification Strategy

| Firm Type | Best Way to Find Recruiters |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Big Tech (Google, Meta, Amazon) | LinkedIn → filter by company + "University Recruiter" or "Technical Recruiter" |
| Big 4 (PwC, EY, KPMG, Deloitte) | LinkedIn → search "[Firm] campus recruiter New York" |
| Hedge Funds / Quant Firms | LinkedIn → company page → People → filter "Recruiter" or "Talent" |
| Startups | LinkedIn + AngelList/Wellfound → "Head of People" or "Talent Acquisition" |
| Consulting | Company website → "Join Us" → university recruiting contacts |
