# STAR Story Bank — Finance, Consulting, Tech

Reference templates for Step 2b. Fill in with candidate's actual details.

## 1. LEADERSHIP STORIES

### Template: Led a Process Change
- **S**: Team/function was doing X the old way, causing Y problem
- **T**: I was responsible for [owned outcome]
- **A**: I identified root cause, proposed solution, got buy-in from [stakeholders], implemented by [specific steps]
- **R**: Reduced [metric] by X%, saving [time/money/errors]

### Template: Managed Competing Priorities Under Pressure
- **S**: Multiple high-stakes deliverables converged — [describe context]
- **T**: I had to sequence and assign work across [team/myself] under a [timeline]
- **A**: Built a priority matrix, escalated risk to [manager/client], executed [steps]
- **R**: All deliverables met on time; [specific outcome]

### Template: Influenced Without Authority
- **S**: Needed [team/function] to adopt a change they didn't own or initiate
- **T**: My role had no direct authority over them
- **A**: Built a business case, ran a pilot, got one champion, then scaled persuasion
- **R**: Change adopted; [metric] improved

## 2. PROBLEM-SOLVING / ANALYTICAL STORIES

### Template: Found Error/Discrepancy Others Missed
- **S**: Routine [report/process] produced [unexpected output]
- **T**: I was responsible for [accuracy/sign-off/review]
- **A**: Traced back to [root cause] by doing [specific analytical steps]
- **R**: Caught $[X] error / prevented regulatory finding / saved [time]

### Template: Built a Model or Tool to Solve a Recurring Problem
- **S**: Team was doing X manually, taking [time], with [error rate]
- **T**: I decided to automate/improve this
- **A**: Built [tool] using [Excel/Python/SQL], tested against [X cases], deployed
- **R**: Time reduced by X%, errors eliminated, now used by [N people]

### Template: Made a Decision With Incomplete Data
- **S**: Had to [recommend/decide] on [X] with limited information
- **T**: Needed to give [stakeholder] a recommendation by [deadline]
- **A**: Used available proxies, stated assumptions clearly, ran sensitivity analysis
- **R**: Recommendation accepted; [outcome confirms decision quality]

## 3. IMPACT / RESULTS STORIES

### Template: Improved a KPI Materially
- **S**: [Metric] was underperforming vs. target/benchmark
- **T**: Owned responsibility for improving it
- **A**: Diagnosed root cause → implemented change → measured results
- **R**: [Metric] improved by [X]% over [Y timeframe]

### Template: Delivered on a High-Visibility Project
- **S**: Organization/client needed [X] — stakes were [high because Y]
- **T**: I was [sole owner / key contributor] of [specific component]
- **A**: [Steps taken]
- **R**: Project delivered [on time/under budget/at quality], recognized by [stakeholder]

---

## 4. CONFLICT / DIFFICULT STAKEHOLDER STORIES

### Template: Disagreed With a Senior Stakeholder and Were Right
- **S**: [Manager/client] wanted to proceed with X; I believed it was wrong because Y
- **T**: My role required me to either comply or escalate professionally
- **A**: Documented my concern, requested a conversation, presented data-backed alternative
- **R**: Decision revised (or: my concern was noted and X was avoided downstream)

### Template: Worked Through a Conflict With a Peer
- **S**: Disagreement with colleague over [approach/ownership/priority]
- **T**: We both owned parts of [shared deliverable]
- **A**: Initiated a direct conversation, separated the professional from the personal, found a shared goal
- **R**: Delivered together successfully; relationship intact

---

## 5. FAILURE / LESSONS LEARNED STORIES

### Template: Made a Mistake and Fixed It
- **S**: I [made an error / missed something] that caused [impact]
- **T**: Responsible for correcting and preventing recurrence
- **A**: Acknowledged quickly, corrected root cause, built a check/process to prevent repeat
- **R**: No recurrence; [stakeholder trust maintained/restored]

> **Coaching note**: Never minimize the mistake — own it fully. The lesson and prevention
> action are what interviewers are actually evaluating. Candidates who say "it was a small
> error" signal low self-awareness.

---

## 6. INITIATIVE / ABOVE-AND-BEYOND STORIES

### Template: Spotted and Acted on Something Outside Your Job Description
- **S**: Noticed a problem/opportunity that wasn't my responsibility
- **T**: Could have ignored it; chose not to
- **A**: Raised it, proposed a fix, executed it (often with no formal mandate)
- **R**: [Outcome] — shows ownership mentality

## FINANCE-SPECIFIC STORY SEEDS

Use these when the target role is credit, FP&A, product control, treasury, or risk:

| Scenario | STAR focus |
|---|---|
| Reconciliation break traced to source | Problem-solving |
| Month-end close under tight deadline | Leadership + results |
| Regulatory reporting error caught pre-submission | Problem-solving + impact |
| Built a dashboard that replaced manual report | Initiative + impact |
| Explained complex variance to senior management | Communication + leadership |
| Onboarded / cross-trained a new team member | Leadership |
| Proposed and implemented a process improvement | Initiative + impact |
| Managed client/counterparty escalation | Conflict / stakeholder |
