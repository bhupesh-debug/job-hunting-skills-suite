## 7. ADVERSARIAL / TRICKY

> **Coaching note**: Adversarial questions exist to test composure, honesty, and self-awareness.
> Defensive or over-rehearsed answers fail. Calm, specific, forward-looking answers win.

## 9. TECHNICAL FINANCE CONCEPTS (Finance Roles)

**Accounting / Control:**
- What's the difference between cash and accrual accounting?
- Explain deferred revenue and when it's recognized.
- What's the impact of a lease capitalization on the three financial statements?
- Walk me through how goodwill impairment affects the income statement and balance sheet.
- What are the main differences between IFRS and US GAAP for financial instruments?

**Risk / Credit:**
- What's duration and convexity in a fixed income context?
- Explain credit default swaps in plain language.
- What's the Basel III framework trying to accomplish?
- What's the difference between VaR and CVaR?
- How do you think about liquidity risk vs. credit risk?

**Valuation / FP&A:**
- Walk me through a DCF.
- What are the limitations of using EV/EBITDA as a valuation multiple?
- How do you think about the cost of equity vs. cost of debt in WACC?
- When would you use precedent transactions vs. comparable companies?
- What's the difference between free cash flow to the firm and free cash flow to equity?
